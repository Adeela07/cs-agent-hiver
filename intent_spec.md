\# AppleSupport Intent Taxonomy



Derived from manual review of 106 real customer messages (see eval/intent\_sample.csv).

Labeling stopped at saturation: last 45 examples introduced zero new categories.



\## software\_bug

Customer reports something broke or misbehaves after a software update (freezing, crashing, glitches, unexpected behavior). Most common intent in this dataset.

Examples:

\- "hey Im having a LOT of issues with iOS 11.0.3 on my 6s (screen freeze, responsiveness issues). Any help?"

\- "why is iOS 11 so horrible?!? Slow, buggy, overheats my iPhone 7."

\- "Hey Messages app instantly crashes. Been running 11.1, this just started."



\## battery\_issue

Battery draining unusually fast, dying at unexpected percentages, or degrading after an update.

Examples:

\- "Ever since I updated to iOS 11, my battery dies at 8%."

\- ".. battery performance has degraded after #ios11update"

\- "why does your latest ios update eat up my battery so quickly?"



\## hardware\_complaint

A physical/mechanical device problem — not caused by software (keyboard, trackpad, charging port, screen).

Examples:

\- "My macbooks trackpad \& keyboard are frozen! How can i fix this?"

\- "hi. I've just opened my refurb Macbook Pro 2017 and it's not charging."



\## device\_troubleshooting

Device won't turn on, won't respond, or needs step-by-step diagnosis (broader than a single named bug).

Examples:

\- "Updated my phone last night and it won't turn back on now."

\- "Great! My Ipad has died, it won't turn on."



\## data\_loss

Photos, contacts, files, or music have disappeared or won't restore.

Examples:

\- "I turned off my phone and when I went into my Camera Roll all my photos are missing."

\- "please explain to me why my contacts keep erasing from my phone."



\## billing\_account

Payments, subscriptions, gift cards, Apple Pay, upgrade programs, account-level issues.

Examples:

\- "Apple Pay Cash is not showing up for me. No pay card in my wallet."

\- "hi, got given an iTunes gift card but the code has peeled off with the label."



\## how\_to

Customer wants to know how to do something — not reporting a problem.

Examples:

\- "hey apple! iphone 6 lock screen. is it normal?"

\- "Do you have a desktop application for Windows10 which manages calendar on iCloud?"



\## general\_complaint

Frustration or anger about service, policy, or experience — not a specific technical bug.

Examples:

\- "What a great way to add to an already outstanding complaint by not phoning me."

\- "Hardware issue on my wifes brand new iPhone 8 and Apple wants me to drive 100+ miles to fix it."



\## unclear

Message is too vague, sarcastic, or incomplete to categorize confidently.

Examples:

\- ".  , explain yourself:"

\- "Fam!? lets discuss"



\## other

Off-topic or outside AppleSupport's typical support domain.

Examples:

\- "where do I find all available episodes of the podcast I already subscribe to?"

\- "why can't these be played? The album is out and I had it preordered"
